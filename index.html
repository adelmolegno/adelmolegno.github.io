About the Artist

Each piece is carefully handcrafted from select hardwoods, bringing out the natural grain and character of the wood. With years of experience in woodworking and sculpture, every creation tells a unique story.

The process begins with selecting the finest wood, then carefully carving and shaping to reveal the hidden beauty within. Each sculpture is finished with natural oils to preserve and enhance the wood's natural luster.
